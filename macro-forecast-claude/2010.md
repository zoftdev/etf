# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2010 → 2011, 2012 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2010** that referred to 2011, 2012 or the following decade. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (-1–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear "hot" commodity → low score or omit.

---

## 1. Top 5 countries (2010 view for 2011/2012+)

From 2010-era reports (IMF WEO Oct 2010, World Bank Global Economic Prospects 2010, Goldman Sachs BRICs / N-11, PwC World in 2050):

| Rank | Country       | Score | Source / note |
|------|---------------|-------|---------------|
| 1    | **China**     | 0.95  | Fastest growth in East Asia (~9.5% 2010 est.); IMF WEO Oct 2010 projected buoyant 2011–12; Goldman Sachs/PwC projected China overtaking US by mid-2020s. |
| 2    | **India**     | 0.90  | South Asia averaging 7.7% 2010–12 (GEP 2010); BRIC; projected among largest economies by 2025 (PwC). |
| 3    | **Brazil**    | 0.85  | BRIC; Latin America leader at ~4.3% annual growth (GEP 2010); top-5 economy by 2050 in Goldman Sachs narrative. |
| 4    | **Indonesia** | 0.75  | East Asia & Pacific standout in GEP 2010 (region at 8.7% 2010); N-11; Goldman Sachs identified Indonesia among best-performing post-crisis. |
| 5    | **Russia**    | 0.70  | BRIC; Europe & Central Asia recovering at 4.1% (GEP 2010); projected among top global economies by 2050 (Goldman Sachs). |

*Alternative 5th: **South Korea** (N-11, strong export recovery) or **Thailand** (East Asia growth leader in GEP 2010).*
