# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2010 → 2021, 2022 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2010** that referred to 2021, 2022 or the following decade. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** &lt; 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear “hot” commodity → low score or omit.

## 1. Top 5 countries (2010 view for 2021/2022+)

From 2010-era reports (IMF WEO Oct 2010, World Bank Global Economic Prospects 2010, Goldman Sachs BRICs / N-11, PwC long-term GDP):

| Rank | Country   | Score | Source / note |
|------|-----------|-------|----------------|
| 1    | **China** | 0.95 | Fastest growth in East Asia (e.g. ~9.5% 2010, 8.5% 2011, 8.2% 2012 in GEP 2010); Goldman/PwC saw China overtaking US in size by mid‑2020s. |
| 2    | **India** | 0.90 | Strong growth (e.g. 8.2–8.7% in GEP 2010); BRIC; projected to be among largest economies by 2025. |
| 3    | **Brazil** | 0.85 | BRIC; Latin America leader in GEP 2010 (e.g. 6.4% 2010); top‑5 economy by 2050 in GS narrative. |
| 4    | **Indonesia** | 0.75 | East Asia & Pacific standout in GEP 2010 (e.g. 5.9–6.3%); N‑11; rising share of global growth. |
| 5    | **Russia**  | 0.70 | BRIC; post‑crisis recovery in 2010–2011 views; projected among top global economies by 2050. |
