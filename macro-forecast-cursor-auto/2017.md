# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2017 → 2018, 2019 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2017** that referred to 2018, 2019 or the following years. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear "hot" commodity → low score or omit.

## 1. Top 5 countries (2017 view for 2018/2019+)

From 2017-era forecasts (World Bank GEP Jan/Jun 2017; IMF WEO Apr/Oct 2017):

| Rank | Country          | Score | Source / note |
|------|------------------|-------|----------------|
| 1    | **India**        | 0.84 | World Bank GEP Jan 2017 projected India sustaining growth above 7% (noting ~7.6% for FY2018); World Bank GEP Jun 2017 projected India accelerating (7.2% FY2017, 7.5% FY2018); IMF WEO 2017 treated India as a core part of emerging Asia’s growth story (even as Oct 2017 revised near-term projections). |
| 2    | **China**        | 0.80 | World Bank GEP Jan 2017 projected China continuing an orderly slowdown to ~6.5%; World Bank GEP Jun 2017 projected ~6.5% in 2017 and ~6.3% in 2018; IMF WEO Oct 2017 cited broad-based upgrades including China/emerging Asia as part of a strengthening global upswing. |
| 3    | **United States**| 0.66 | World Bank GEP Jan 2017 expected US growth picking up to ~2.2% with manufacturing/investment gaining traction; IMF WEO Apr 2017 highlighted a cyclical recovery in investment/manufacturing/trade supporting 2017–2018 growth; IMF WEO Oct 2017 described a strengthening global upswing including the US. |
| 4    | **Indonesia**    | 0.58 | World Bank GEP Jan 2017 noted Indonesia picking up (supported by rising private investment); World Bank GEP Jun 2017 projected Indonesia ~5.2% in 2017 and ~5.3% in 2018, supported by improving external demand and modestly rising commodity prices; IMF WEO Oct 2017 highlighted emerging Asia upgrades as part of the upswing. |
| 5    | **Brazil**       | 0.52 | World Bank GEP Jan 2017 expected commodity exporters to rebound as obstacles recede and explicitly flagged Russia/Brazil resuming growth after recessions; World Bank GEP Jun 2017 projected Brazil emerging from recession (~0.3% in 2017; ~1.8% in 2018); IMF WEO Oct 2017 noted commodity exporters still adjusting but with growth strengthening in parts of EM. |

*Alternative 5th from 2017 narratives: **Russia** (WB GEP Jun 2017; IMF WEO Oct 2017 upgrades for emerging Europe/Russia).*

## 2. Top commodity (2017 view for 2018/2019+)

From **World Bank Commodity Markets Outlook** (Jan, Oct 2017) and **IMF WEO 2017** commodity focus:

| Commodity              | Score | Note |
|------------------------|-------|------|
| **Crude oil / energy** | 0.82 | World Bank CMO Jan 2017 held crude oil forecast at ~$55/bbl for 2017 (rebound from 2016) on supply tightening and expected partial OPEC compliance; World Bank CMO Oct 2017 projected oil averaging ~$56/bbl in 2018 on steady demand + production cuts. |
| Metals                 | 0.58 | World Bank CMO Jan 2017 raised metals forecast (first rise in years) on tightening supply and strong demand (incl. China); World Bank CMO Oct 2017 expected metals surge to level off after a strong 2017 jump, with mixed base-metal dynamics (iron ore down; others supported by tight supply). |
| Agriculture / food     | 0.30 | World Bank CMO Jan 2017 expected agriculture broadly flat-to-slightly up (with grains down, others modest); World Bank CMO Oct 2017 expected agriculture edging up in 2018 with well-supplied markets and only marginal price increases. |

*Commodity theme in 2017 was “industrial commodities rebound” — energy led; metals strong; agriculture comparatively subdued.*
