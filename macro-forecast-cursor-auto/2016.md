# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2016 → 2017, 2018 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2016** that referred to 2017, 2018 or the following years. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear "hot" commodity → low score or omit.

## 1. Top 5 countries (2016 view for 2017/2018+)

From 2016-era reports (IMF WEO Apr/Oct 2016; World Bank GEP Jan/Jun 2016):

| Rank | Country        | Score | Source / note |
|------|----------------|-------|----------------|
| 1    | **India**      | 0.84 | IMF WEO Oct 2016: emerging Asia (India in particular) showing robust growth; World Bank GEP Jun 2016 press release: India expected to hold steady at 7.6% growth in 2016 with strong domestic demand supporting outlook into 2017. |
| 2    | **China**      | 0.78 | IMF WEO Apr 2016: China slowdown/rebalancing central to global outlook; World Bank GEP Jun 2016 press release: China forecast ~6.7% in 2016 (policy support + orderly slowdown assumption), key driver of regional spillovers. |
| 3    | **Indonesia**  | 0.62 | World Bank GEP Jun 2016 press release (regional outlook): rising investment expected to support activity in Indonesia (alongside Malaysia/Thailand); IMF WEO Apr/Oct 2016 include Indonesia in EMDE projections tables and discuss Asia resilience amid capital flow and trade slowdown. |
| 4    | **Brazil**     | 0.56 | IMF WEO 2016 emphasized stress in large commodity exporters; World Bank GEP Jun 2016 press release: Brazil projected to remain in deep recession in 2016 with weakness carrying into 2017, a major EM drag and regional spillover source. |
| 5    | **Russia**     | 0.50 | IMF WEO 2016: adjusting to lower commodity prices and weak growth in commodity exporters; World Bank GEP Jun 2016 press release: Russia projected to remain in deeper recession than forecast in January (commodity price adjustment + sanctions/geopolitics), affecting Europe & Central Asia. |

*Alternative 5th from 2016 narratives: **South Africa** (low growth cited in WB GEP Jun 2016) or **Mexico** (resilience via US ties in WB GEP Jun 2016).*

## 2. Top commodity (2016 view for 2017/2018+)

From **World Bank Commodity Markets Outlook** (Jan, Oct 2016) and **IMF WEO 2016** commodity focus:

| Commodity              | Score | Note |
|------------------------|-------|------|
| **Crude oil / energy** | 0.84 | World Bank CMO Jan 2016 cut 2016 oil forecast to $37/bbl; CMO Oct 2016 raised 2017 oil forecast to $55/bbl with OPEC supply limits; IMF WEO Apr 2016 highlighted further commodity price declines (esp. oil) as a major global realignment. |
| Metals                 | 0.50 | World Bank CMO Oct 2016 projected modest metals/minerals recovery in 2017 (+4.1%) with zinc forecast +20%+; IMF WEO 2016 covered broader commodity price effects on exporters and investment. |
| Agriculture / food     | 0.35 | World Bank CMO Jan 2016 projected agriculture prices slightly down in 2016; CMO Oct 2016 projected modest increase in 2017; still secondary versus energy in 2016 outlooks. |

*Commodity theme in 2016 was still oil/energy (bottoming + expected recovery); others were secondary.*
