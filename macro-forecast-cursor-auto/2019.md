# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2019 → 2020, 2021 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2019** that referred to 2020, 2021 or the following years. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear "hot" commodity → low score or omit.

## 2. Top commodity (2019 view for 2020/2021+)

From **World Bank Commodity Markets Outlook** (Apr, Oct 2019) and **IMF WEO 2019**:

| Commodity              | Score | Note |
|------------------------|-------|------|
| **Crude oil / energy** | 0.80 | World Bank CMO Apr 2019: crude ~$66/bbl (2019), ~$65 (2020); CMO Oct 2019 revised down to $60 (2019), $58 (2020) on weaker global growth and ample supplies; energy prices ~15% lower in 2019 than 2018, declining into 2020; IMF WEO Oct 2019 highlighted global manufacturing downturn and trade barriers affecting demand. |
| Metals                 | 0.48 | World Bank CMO Oct 2019: metals projected to fall 5% in 2019 and continue to slide in 2020 on slowing demand; precious metals expected to gain in 2020 on uncertainty and accommodative monetary policy; secondary to energy in 2019 narrative. |
| Agriculture / food     | 0.38 | World Bank CMO Oct 2019: agriculture prices to decline in 2019 but stabilize in 2020; trade tension resolution could lift soybeans/corn; secondary to energy and metals. |

*Commodity theme in 2019 was oil/energy (revisions down on weak growth); no single "hot" commodity — oil still most cited.*
