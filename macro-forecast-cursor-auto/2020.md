# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2020 → 2021, 2022 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2020** that referred to 2021, 2022 or the medium-term recovery after COVID. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear “hot” commodity → low score or omit.

## 1. Top 5 countries (2020 view for 2021/2022+)

From 2020-era global outlooks (IMF WEO Oct 2020; World Bank GEP Jun 2020) emphasizing post‑pandemic rebound with Asia recovering fastest:

| Rank | Country | Score | Source / note |
|------|---------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | **China** | 0.86 | IMF WEO Oct 2020 projected China as one of the few economies with **positive 2020 growth (1.9%)** and a strong **2021 rebound (8.2%)**; World Bank GEP Jun 2020 highlighted China’s early restart as a key driver in the global recovery narrative. |
| 2 | **India** | 0.82 | IMF WEO Oct 2020 projected a deep **2020 contraction (-10.3%)** followed by a sharp **2021 rebound (8.8%)** (base effects + reopening); World Bank GEP Jun 2020 framed South Asia’s recovery outlook around India’s rebound path and pandemic risks. |
| 3 | **United States** | 0.62 | IMF WEO Oct 2020 projected **-4.3% (2020)** then **+3.1% (2021)**; World Bank GEP Jun 2020 “major economies” section treated the US recovery pace, policy support, and uncertainty as central to the global baseline. |
| 4 | **Indonesia** | 0.54 | IMF WEO Oct 2020 projected **-1.5% (2020)** then **+6.1% (2021)**; World Bank GEP Jun 2020 described emerging‑market recoveries (incl. commodity exporters in EAP) as rebound‑but‑risk‑heavy in 2021. |
| 5 | **Brazil** | 0.46 | IMF WEO Oct 2020 projected **-5.8% (2020)** then **+2.8% (2021)**; World Bank GEP Jun 2020 Latin America outlook emphasized a hard hit in 2020 and a partial rebound in 2021 constrained by health and financial conditions. |

## 2. Top commodity (2020 view for 2021/2022+)

Energy/oil dominated 2020 macro outlooks because of the historic demand shock and partial recovery expectations.

| Commodity | Score | Note |
|----------|-------|------|
| **Crude oil / energy** | 0.78 | World Bank Commodity Markets Outlook (Oct 2020) expected oil to average **$44/bbl in 2021** (up from ~$41 in 2020) but remain below pre‑pandemic levels; FactSet’s CY2021 S&P 500 earnings preview (Dec 2020) noted higher **expected average oil price in 2021 (~$45.75)** supporting an earnings rebound in the US Energy sector. |

## 3. US sector (2020 view for 2021/2022+)

Late‑2020 equity outlooks leaned toward a **re‑opening / cyclical rebound** (industrials, discretionary, energy) alongside ongoing policy support.

| Sector | Score | Note |
|--------|-------|------|
| **Industrials** | 0.72 | FactSet (Dec 2020) projected **Industrials** to have the **largest 2021 earnings growth (78.0%)** among S&P 500 sectors; UBS (Nov 2020) highlighted upside potential in 2021 for cyclical exposures including **industrial** companies as the economy reopens. |
| **Consumer Discretionary** | 0.62 | FactSet (Dec 2020) projected **Consumer Discretionary** earnings growth of **58.9%** in CY2021; UBS (Nov 2020) included **consumer discretionary** among areas with upside in a “renewal” year as stimulus + reopening lift demand. |
