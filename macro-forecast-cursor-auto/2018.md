# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2018 → 2019, 2020 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2018** that referred to 2019, 2020 or the following years. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear "hot" commodity → low score or omit.

## 1. Top 5 countries (2018 view for 2019/2020+)

From 2018-era forecasts (World Bank GEP Jan/Jun 2018; IMF WEO Apr/Oct 2018):

| Rank | Country          | Score | Source / note |
|------|------------------|-------|----------------|
| 1    | **India**        | 0.86 | World Bank GEP Jun 2018 projected India at ~7.3% (FY2018/19) and ~7.5% (FY2019/20) with robust private consumption and strengthening investment; IMF WEO Apr 2018 emphasized strong growth in emerging Asia through 2018–2019. |
| 2    | **China**        | 0.78 | World Bank GEP Jan 2018 projected China slowing structurally (with China growth moderating, e.g. ~6.4% in 2018 noted in regional summary); IMF WEO Oct 2018 described global growth becoming less balanced and revised down outlook, with major economies (incl. China) expected to decelerate. |
| 3    | **United States**| 0.64 | IMF WEO Apr 2018 explicitly cited expansionary fiscal policy in the United States supporting global growth in 2018–2019; World Bank GEP Jan/Jun 2018 expected advanced economy growth to moderate as central banks remove accommodation, implying a late‑cycle but still key US driver into 2019. |
| 4    | **Indonesia**    | 0.56 | World Bank GEP Jan 2018 projected Indonesia accelerating to ~5.3% in 2018 (from ~5.1% in 2017); World Bank GEP Jun 2018 reiterated Indonesia ~5.2% in 2018 and ~5.3% in 2019 amid robust EMDE momentum (with IMF WEO Apr/Oct 2018 also highlighting emerging Asia’s strength). |
| 5    | **Brazil**       | 0.50 | World Bank GEP Jan 2018 projected Brazil picking up to ~2.0% in 2018 (from ~1.0% in 2017) as the cyclical recovery continued; World Bank GEP Jun 2018 forecast Brazil ~2.4% in 2018 and ~2.5% in 2019, consistent with IMF WEO Apr 2018 noting recovery signs in several commodity exporters. |

*Alternative 5th from 2018 narratives: **Russia** (World Bank GEP Jun 2018 projected Russia holding ~1.5% in 2018 and ~1.8% in 2019 on higher oil prices, albeit constrained by sanctions/production cuts).*
