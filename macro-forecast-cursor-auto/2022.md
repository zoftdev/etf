# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2022 → 2023, 2024 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2022** that referred to 2023, 2024 or the medium term. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear “hot” commodity → low score or omit.

## 2. Top commodity (2022 view for 2023/2024+)

Energy dominated 2022 outlooks: war in Ukraine, oil and gas volatility, and CMO projections for 2023.

| Commodity | Score | Note |
|-----------|-------|------|
| **Crude oil / energy** | 0.84 | World Bank CMO Oct 2022: energy prices surged ~60% in 2022, projected to decline 11% in 2023 but remain 75% above five-year average; Brent ~$92/bbl in 2023; CMO Apr 2022 had oil ~$100/bbl 2022. Clearly “hot” in 2022. |
| **Agriculture / food** | 0.58 | World Bank CMO Oct 2022: food-price inflation very high in South Asia (>20%) and 12–15% in LAC, MENA, SSA, ECA; food crisis and Ukraine export disruptions; agricultural prices projected to decline 5% in 2023 but risks remained. |
| **Metals** | 0.42 | World Bank CMO Oct 2022: metals projected to decline 15% in 2023 on weaker growth and China slowdown; special focus on copper and aluminum; secondary to energy/food in 2022 narratives. |

## 3. US sector (2022 view for 2023/2024+)

Late-2022 equity outlooks for 2023 favored **defensive sectors** (healthcare, staples, utilities) and cited **Consumer Discretionary** for earnings leadership; **Energy** was 2022’s top performer.

| Sector | Score | Note |
|--------|-------|------|
| **Healthcare** | 0.76 | Business Insider Dec 2022: Healthcare among Wall Street’s most favored sectors for 2023; defensive, recession-resistant, low rate sensitivity; Morningstar late 2022 2023 outlook cited defensive positioning. |
| **Consumer Discretionary** | 0.64 | FactSet Dec 2022 CY 2023 earnings preview: Consumer Discretionary expected to lead S&P 500 sector earnings growth in 2023; modest overall growth (0.6–5.5%) with this sector leading. |
| **Energy** | 0.62 | Energy was the only S&P 500 sector with positive returns in 2022 (+58%); late-2022 outlooks noted elevated oil/commodity prices and volatility as ongoing theme into 2023. |
