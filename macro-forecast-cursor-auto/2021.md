# Macro forecast & outlook (views from 2021 → 2022, 2023 & beyond)

Forecasts and outlooks **published in 2021** that referred to 2022, 2023 or the medium-term recovery. Used to extract: top 5 countries, top commodity, US sector.

**Scoring (0–1):** < 0.3 = not much — remove or set low; use score to prioritise. No clear “hot” commodity → low score or omit.

---

## 1. Top 5 countries (2021 view for 2022/2023+)

From 2021-era global outlooks (IMF WEO Oct 2021; World Bank GEP Jun 2021) emphasizing uneven recovery, with major economies (US, China) and emerging Asia leading:

| Rank | Country | Score | Source / note |
|------|---------|-------|----------------|
| 1 | **China** | 0.84 | World Bank GEP Jun 2021 projected China **8.5% growth in 2021** as a key driver of global recovery; IMF WEO Oct 2021 highlighted divergences with advanced/EM and China among major economies with early restart and policy support. |
| 2 | **India** | 0.80 | IMF WEO Oct 2021 and World Bank GEP Jun 2021 both emphasized **emerging Asia** as growth engine; India central to South Asia and EM narrative; GEP Jun 2021 noted strong but uneven growth with many developing countries lagging. |

## 2. Top commodity (2021 view for 2022/2023+)

Energy dominated 2021 outlooks: oil and natural gas prices surged; World Bank CMO highlighted energy as main focus.

| Commodity | Score | Note |
|-----------|-------|------|
| **Crude oil / energy** | 0.82 | World Bank CMO Oct 2021 projected oil averaging **$74/bbl in 2022** (up from $70 in 2021) and emphasized “soaring energy prices” and inflation risks; CMO Apr 2021 noted commodity prices stabilizing after early 2021 gains on global recovery — energy clearly “hot” in 2021 narratives. |

## 3. US sector (2021 view for 2022/2023+)

Late-2021 equity outlooks for 2022 favored **Energy** (earnings rebound, oil prices), **Financials** (rising rates), and **Industrials** (earnings growth).

| Sector | Score | Note |
|--------|-------|------|
| **Energy** | 0.80 | FactSet Dec 2021 CY 2022 earnings preview projected **Energy** with **151.7% YoY earnings growth** and as the largest contributor to S&P 500 growth (ex-Energy, index would show decline); Kiplinger/WS consensus Dec 2021 cited Energy as top 2021 performer (48.5%) and favored for 2022. |
| **Financials** | 0.70 | Kiplinger “Where to Invest in 2022” (Dec 2021) and Wall Street consensus cited **Financials** on rising rates and robust growth; FactSet 2022 outlook context; major banks (Goldman, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley) overweight financials for 2022. |
| **Industrials** | 0.64 | FactSet Dec 2021 projected **Industrials** with **27.4% earnings growth** for CY 2022, among the top sector growth rates. |
