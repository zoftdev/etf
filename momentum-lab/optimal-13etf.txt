  Insights by parameter:

  n_long — 5 is the winner, not 4 (QuantPedia default)
  - Top 7 Sharpe configs all use n_long=5
  - n_long=2 gives highest CAGR (11.9%) but worst drawdowns (-34% to -43%)
  - n_long=5 is the sweet spot: slightly less return, much better risk-adjusted

  Short hedge — Small or none is better
  - short_weight=0 or 0.20 dominates top spots
  - Default 0.30 is mediocre, 0.40-0.50 actively hurts performance
  - The hedge protects drawdown slightly but costs return

  Momentum periods — Adding 21-day (1-month) helps
  - (21,63,126,189,252) — the 5-period extended variant — appears in top Sharpe configs
  - Single-period (63,) or (126,) still competitive on CAGR but worse risk-adjusted

  Correlation window — Shorter (10-day) works better
  - corr_short_days=10 beats default 20: Sharpe 0.75 vs 0.67
  - corr_short_days=30 actively hurts (Sharpe 0.56)
